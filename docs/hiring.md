# DonaLabs Hiring Narrative

## DonaLabs

DonaLabs is a venture studio where engineers, designers, and builders collaborate to launch new digital products. We explore ideas quickly, validate opportunities, and move from concept to production with high ownership.

Our teams work across multiple products and markets. We care about pragmatic execution, strong product intuition, and building scalable systems that can support real users.

### Mission and Impact

- Build products that solve real-world problems
- Turn validated ideas into usable, high-quality software
- Combine speed with engineering depth to create durable platforms

### Why Join Us

- High ownership from discovery through delivery
- Opportunity to shape architecture early, not just maintain it
- Exposure to multiple products, domains, and growth stages
- Foundry-style culture: evaluate, invest, and build

## PsonalHealth

PsonalHealth is a modern healthcare platform focused on improving how patients and providers connect. We build secure, intuitive tools that streamline workflows and support better care experiences.

The product direction combines strong engineering practices with thoughtful UX, aiming to make healthcare technology more personal, accessible, and human-centered.

### Mission and Impact

- Improve care coordination through better digital workflows
- Reduce operational friction for providers
- Deliver trustworthy, user-friendly experiences in healthcare contexts

### Why This Work Matters

Healthcare software affects real people under real constraints. Building reliable, understandable systems here creates meaningful day-to-day impact for both patients and care teams.
